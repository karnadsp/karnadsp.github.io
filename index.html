About

I'm a Health Informatics graduate of the University of Michigan (MHI, Machine Learning & Data Analytics, GPA 3.936) and a Biomedical Engineer by training. My work sits at the intersection of clinical AI, healthcare data systems, and translational population health analytics.

At the Biocomplexity Institute at Indiana University, I designed and deployed a 9-agent CrewAI pipeline for automated biomedical data processing which is one of the more technically complex agentic AI systems I've built, involving custom Python wrappers, LLM inference cost optimization, and zero-shot failure mode documentation.

That clinical grounding shapes how I approach data problems: I understand what the data represents before I analyze it.
