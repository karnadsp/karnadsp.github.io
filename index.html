About

I'm a Health Informatics graduate of the University of Michigan (MHI, Machine Learning & Data Analytics) and a Biomedical Engineer by training. My work sits at the intersection of clinical AI, healthcare data systems, and translational health analytics.

At the Biocomplexity Institute at Indiana University, I designed and deployed a 9-agent CrewAI pipeline for biological simulation code generation which is one of the more technically complex agentic AI systems I've built. My academic projects span SDOH-driven sepsis risk modeling, AI hallucination governance in EHR systems, diabetes risk stratification, and FHIR-interoperable clinical data pipelines.

Before Michigan, I spent two years at Siamaf Healthcare building ML models for surgical oncology clinical decision support and conducting primary research with clinical teams. My biomedical engineering background gives me a clinical grounding most data scientists don't have: I understand what the data represents before I analyze it.

I'm authorized to work in the US under F-1 OPT beginning June 22, 2026, with a 3-year window under STEM OPT extension eligibility.
